\title{\textbf{Separating the completeness budget from its spatial placement:\\
the aggregate-$C$ completion in \texttt{darksirens}}\\[4pt]
\large Technical note accompanying PR \#338 (\texttt{feat/completeness-aggregate-budget})}
\author{Ignacio Maga\~na Hernandez}
\date{August 8, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The dark-siren redshift prior completes a magnitude-limited galaxy catalog with a
missing-galaxy branch whose differential completeness $C$ was estimated per HEALPix
pixel from the observed counts themselves. Because the estimator divides a per-pixel
numerator by an isotropic expectation, it measures the product of the survey selection
and the local galaxy overdensity, $C \simeq C_{\rm sel}(z)\,[1+\delta_{\rm obs}(p,z)]$,
clipped to $[0,1]$: angular clustering is absorbed into the ``how much is missing''
budget, and the predicted missing density \emph{anti-correlates} with the true one.
This note derives the failure, documents the corrected estimators introduced in
PR~\#338 --- a sky-aggregate budget $\Cbar(z)$ that reserves all spatial structure for
the completion field $\Qlss(p,z)$, a per-redshift mean-one renormalization that makes
the missing budget invariant to $Q$-fit noise by construction, a matched truncation of
the expected counts at the survey depth, and a resolution guard on the low-rank
Gaussian-process completion --- and quantifies every change on a closure experiment
with known injected clustering and on an end-to-end $H_0$ scan of 300 mock dark
sirens. All legacy behavior is preserved bit-for-bit at default settings; the new
estimators are opt-in and provenance-locked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup and notation}

Let $z_g$ denote the package redshift grid ($N_g$ log-spaced nodes on $[0, z_{\max}]$)
and $p = 1,\dots,N_{\rm pix}$ the HEALPix pixels of solid angle
$a_{\rm pix} = 4\pi/N_{\rm pix}$. The homogeneous expectation for the observable
galaxy density per pixel per unit redshift is
\begin{equation}
\dNexp(z) \;=\; n_0\, a_{\rm pix}\, \frac{dV_c}{dz\,d\Omega}(z)\,(1+z)^{\delta},
\label{eq:dnexp}
\end{equation}
with $n_0$ the comoving density and $\delta$ a phenomenological evolution index; both
are sampled in the hierarchical likelihood. Every density that enters a ratio is
smoothed by one linear operator $S$, built from unit-mass truncated Gaussian kernels
of width $\sigma_s = 0.05$: the observed-galaxy KDE in pixel $p$ is
\begin{equation}
\dNobs(z\,|\,p) \;=\; \sum_{i \in p} \frac{\mathcal{N}(z;\, z_i, \sigma_s)}{M(z_i)},
\qquad
M(z_i) = \Phi\!\left(\tfrac{z_{\max}-z_i}{\sigma_s}\right) - \Phi\!\left(\tfrac{-z_i}{\sigma_s}\right),
\label{eq:kde}
\end{equation}
and the matched expectation is $\dNexps = S\,\dNexp$. Because $S$ is shared and
linear, a redshift-independent true completeness passes through the ratio exactly.

The assembled per-pixel redshift prior is the in/out mixture
\begin{equation}
p(z\,|\,p) \;=\; \frac{N_{\rm obs}(p)\, p_{\rm cat}(z\,|\,p) \;+\; \dNmiss(z\,|\,p)}
{N_{\rm obs}(p) + N_{\rm miss}(p)},
\qquad
N_{\rm miss}(p) = \int \dNmiss(z\,|\,p)\, dz ,
\label{eq:prior}
\end{equation}
with $p_{\rm cat}$ the unit-normalized catalog kernel density and $N_{\rm obs}$ the
observed count. In \emph{conditional} sky weighting every pixel carries unit mass; in
\emph{field} weighting the denominator is replaced by the survey-global
$Z = \sum_p [N_{\rm obs}(p) + N_{\rm miss}(p)]$, restoring relative angular host
density. The missing branch is
\begin{equation}
\dNmiss(z\,|\,p) \;=\; \bigl[1 - C(z\,|\,p)\bigr]\, \dNexp(z)\, \ell(p, z),
\label{eq:dnmiss}
\end{equation}
where the large-scale-structure factor $\ell$ is exactly one of
\begin{equation}
\ell(p,z) \;=\;
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{homogeneous},\\[2pt]
\max\!\bigl(1 + b_{\rm eff}\,\delta_g(p,z),\, 0\bigr) & \text{legacy local overdensity},\\[2pt]
\Qlss(p,z) = e^{\log Q(p,z)} & \text{precomputed completion field},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:lss}
\end{equation}
the latter two mutually exclusive. Beyond a declared survey depth $z_{\rm depth}$ the
convention is $C \equiv 0$, $\ell \equiv 1$ (hosts are missing, not nonexistent).

\section{The legacy per-pixel estimator and its failure modes}
\label{sec:legacy}

\subsection{Clustering absorption (the thinning degeneracy)}

The legacy differential completeness is estimated per pixel,
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm pp}(z\,|\,p) \;=\;
\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\dNobs(z\,|\,p)}{\dNexps(z)},\; 0,\; 1\right).
\label{eq:cpp}
\end{equation}
The numerator is per-pixel data; the denominator is isotropic. Writing the observed
density as selection times local structure,
$\dNobs \simeq S\,[\,C_{\rm sel}\,\dNexp\,(1+\delta_{\rm obs})\,]$, the estimator
returns
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm pp}(z\,|\,p) \;\simeq\;
\mathrm{clip}\!\bigl( C_{\rm sel}(z)\,[1+\delta_{\rm obs}(p,z)],\, 0,\, 1 \bigr):
\label{eq:cpp-absorb}
\end{equation}
the angular clustering of the \emph{observed} galaxies is absorbed into the
completeness. This is the classical thinning (selection--density) degeneracy of an
inhomogeneous Poisson process: from counts alone only the product
$\lambda = \phi \cdot n$ is identifiable, and splitting it requires external
structure. Galaxy surveys avoid counts-based completeness for precisely this reason
(injection campaigns and survey-property templates are constructed to be independent
of the real density field); among dark-siren pipelines the counts-based per-pixel
construction appears in the DarkSirensStat mask/cone completeness, with the
absorption pathology acknowledged there.

Two immediate consequences follow from Eq.~\eqref{eq:cpp-absorb}. First, an overdense
\emph{complete} pixel reads $C > 1$ and is clipped: its excess is silently discarded.
Second, and structurally worse, the missing branch anti-tracks the truth: to first
order in $\delta$,
\begin{equation}
1 - C_{\rm pp} \;\simeq\; (1 - C_{\rm sel}) - C_{\rm sel}\,\delta_{\rm obs},
\end{equation}
so a pixel with \emph{more} true (and hence more missing) galaxies is assigned
\emph{fewer} predicted missing galaxies. On the closure mock (Sec.~\ref{sec:closure})
the measured anomaly correlation of $\log \dNmiss$ against truth is $r = -0.43$ with
slope $-0.62$. In the assembled prior~\eqref{eq:prior} the observed and missing
branches then largely cancel the catalog's clustering (total-density slope $0.25$
against truth), and because the completion field $\Qlss$ is fit to the \emph{same}
counts residual to $C_{\rm pp}$ (its Poisson base is $C_{\rm pp}\,\dNexp$), it can
only ever recover sub-smoothing structure --- measured slope $0.29$, against $0.77$
for the crude legacy $\delta_g$ factor.

\subsection{Jensen inflation of the missing budget}

The completion tables store the Laplace posterior mean
$\mathbb{E}[Q] = \exp( b\,s_{\rm MAP} - \text{shift} + \tfrac12 b^2 \sigma^2_{\rm post})$.
The posterior variance $\sigma^2_{\rm post}$ is largest exactly where data are sparse,
so the spatially varying lognormal factor $e^{\sigma^2/2}$ does not integrate away:
on the mock the radial-mode missing budget is inflated by $+55\%$ relative to the
homogeneous budget. The budget does not belong to $Q$ at all --- it is the job of
$C$ and the sampled $n_0$.

\subsection{Resolution collapse of the low-rank completion field}

The 3-D completion field is a low-rank Gaussian process on (sphere $\times$
$\zeta = \log(1+z)$) with $M_z$ inducing nodes spanning $[0, \zeta_{\rm hi}]$. The
shipped hyperparameters fixed the correlation length at $50\,$Mpc, which maps to
$\ell_\zeta = L\,({d\zeta}/{d\chi})|_{z_{\rm ref}} \approx 0.01$, roughly $30\times$
\emph{below} the node spacing $\zeta_{\rm hi}/(M_z - 1) \approx 0.36$. In this regime
a variational/low-rank GP posterior collapses toward its prior while the optimizer
converges normally (Burt et al.\ 2019): the fitted field is nearly blind to real
radial structure (measured fitted-vs-truth $\log Q$ slope $0.04$), with no
configuration error raised and no CLI knob available to change the lengthscale.

\subsection{Depth-edge mismatch}

The galaxy kernels~\eqref{eq:kde} renormalize on $[0, z_{\max}]$ of the grid, while
the expected side smoothed the \emph{untruncated} $\dNexp$. Just below a declared
depth $z_{\rm depth}$ the numerator loses the kernel mass beyond the edge but the
denominator does not, so $C$ dips and
$\dNmiss = (1-C)\,\dNexp$ spikes within $\sim 2\sigma_s$ of the edge.

\section{The corrected estimators (PR \#338)}
\label{sec:new}

\subsection{S0a: matched truncation at the survey depth}

With a concrete $z_{\rm depth}$ the expected side is truncated identically to the
observed side before smoothing,
\begin{equation}
\dNexps(z) \;=\; \bigl[S\,\bigl(\dNexp \cdot \mathbf{1}[z \le z_{\rm depth}]\bigr)\bigr](z),
\end{equation}
so a constant true completeness again passes through the ratio exactly at the edge
(the same construction the $z=0$ boundary already used). Beyond the depth the
denominator is pinned to an inert constant: the truncated smooth underflows there,
and a live $\text{data}/\epsilon$ division feeds $0 \times \infty$ NaN cotangents
through the downstream \texttt{clip}/\texttt{where} in reverse-mode differentiation
even though every consumer discards $C$ beyond the depth. The $z_{\rm depth} = $
\texttt{None} path is bit-identical to the legacy expression.

\subsection{S0b: per-redshift mean-one budget renormalization}

After any table build (radial or gp3d), with build-time weights
$w_p(z) = [1 - C_p(z)]\,\dNexp(z)$ over the fitted footprint $\mathcal{F}$,
\begin{equation}
\log Q_p(z) \;\longleftarrow\; \log Q_p(z) \;-\;
\log \frac{\sum_{p' \in \mathcal{F}} w_{p'}(z)\, Q_{p'}(z)}
{\sum_{p' \in \mathcal{F}} w_{p'}(z)} ,
\label{eq:renorm}
\end{equation}
applied to the MAP table \emph{and independently to every Laplace ensemble member}.
The identity $\sum_p w_p Q_p = \sum_p w_p$ then holds exactly per redshift bin: $Q$
becomes a pure \emph{redistribution} field, the missing budget with any $Q$ equals
the homogeneous budget by construction (killing the $+55\%$ Jensen inflation
identically), and the ensemble carries placement uncertainty but zero budget
uncertainty --- the budget lives in $C$ and the sampled $n_0$, where it is
marginalized. The removed monopole curve and a boolean stamp are recorded in the
HDF5 provenance (fail-closed writer; loud loader warning for unstamped legacy
tables). The invariant is defined over the \emph{fitted} footprint: occupied pixels
for the per-pixel base, the whole sky for the aggregate base; the gp3d
``borrowing halo'' evaluated onto unfitted pixels deliberately sits outside it.

\subsection{S0c: hyperparameter exposure and the resolution guard}

The builder gains \texttt{--lss-corr-length-mpc}, \texttt{--lss-sigma},
\texttt{--gp3d-nz-nodes}, \texttt{--gp3d-nsph-nodes}, and \texttt{--gp3d-z-node-hi}
(default moved from a hardwired $\zeta_{\rm hi} = \log 4$ to the grid maximum). A
\emph{hard error} fires at build time when
\begin{equation}
\frac{\zeta_{\rm hi}}{M_z - 1} \;>\; \ell_\zeta ,
\end{equation}
stating both numbers and the minimum admissible $M_z$; the sphere side warns when
$\sqrt{4\pi/M_{\rm sph}} > \ell_{\rm sph}$. The configuration that produced the
slope-$0.04$ collapse can no longer build silently.

\subsection{S1: the aggregate budget $\Cbar$}

The opt-in mode \texttt{c\_mode="aggregate"} replaces the per-pixel
ratio~\eqref{eq:cpp} by one sky-aggregate curve per likelihood proposal,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\;
\Cbar(z) \;=\;
\mathrm{clip}\!\left(
\frac{\displaystyle \sum_{p} \dNobs(z\,|\,p)}
{\,N_{\rm pix}^{\rm tot}\; \dNexps(z)\,},\; 0,\; 1\right),
\qquad
N_{\rm pix}^{\rm tot} = \mathrm{round}\!\left(\frac{4\pi}{a_{\rm pix}}\right),
\;\;}
\label{eq:cbar}
\end{equation}
broadcast identically to every pixel (occupied \emph{and} empty) in both the
conditional and field paths, so numerator and global normalizer carry the same
budget by construction. The numerator is a $\theta$-independent data constant
(reusing the existing full-sky KDE rows; a compact catalog without them is refused
rather than silently under-counted); all parameter dependence enters through the
shared denominator. Averaged over the footprint the density fluctuations cancel,
$\sum_p \delta_p \approx 0$, so $\Cbar$ estimates the \emph{selection alone}: on the
mock it matches the analytic generative selection to $0.3\%$ in median absolute
deviation. The missing branch becomes
\begin{equation}
\dNmiss(z\,|\,p) \;=\; \bigl[1 - \Cbar(z)\bigr]\, \dNexp(z)\, \ell(p, z),
\end{equation}
with $(1-\Cbar)$ pixel-independent: the entire spatial signal is carried by $\ell$,
whose correlation with the truth flips positive by construction. Correspondingly the
completion-field fit conditions on the base $\Cbar\,\dNexp$ and \emph{includes empty
pixels} as $N_{\rm obs} = 0$ rows against the nonzero base --- the zero count is the
void information the occupied-only fit discarded. $Q$ now targets the full observed
overdensity rather than the sub-smoothing residual. Tables record their
\texttt{c\_mode} in provenance and consumption is fail-closed in both directions
(the two targets differ by the whole clustering signal; mixing them double-counts or
drops it).

Two documented caveats. $\Cbar \propto 1/n_0$ through a single global clip, so the
likelihood can be kinked in the sampled $n_0$ near full completeness. And the
sky-aggregate assumption presumes isotropic survey depth; for genuinely anisotropic
compilations the sanctioned extensions are stratified $\Cbar_k$ per depth region or
a magnitude-based $C_{\rm sel}(z;\theta)$ fit from the per-galaxy magnitude
likelihood (which is independent of the density field by thinning) --- staged as
future work, not part of this PR.

\subsection{Auxiliary fix: grid endpoint vs.\ distance table}

Under a non-default \texttt{DARKSIRENS\_ZMAX}, XLA's and NumPy's
$\mathrm{expm1}\circ\log$ can disagree by one ulp (e.g.\ at $z_{\max} = 0.52$), which
left the redshift grid's last node \emph{above} the NumPy-built distance table; the
out-of-range mask then returned $\mathrm{NaN}$ for $r(z_{\max})$ and poisoned every
consumer of $\chi(z_g)$ (measured: an all-NaN radial $Q$ table, refused by the
fail-closed writer). The endpoint is now clamped to the NumPy value when lower ---
bit-inert at the default $z_{\max} = 5$.

\section{Validation}
\label{sec:closure}

\subsection{End-to-end $H_0$ scan}

300 GW dark sirens hosted by the clustered catalog with a DAG-consistent pipeline
(detection cut on the observed SNR; the PE conditions on the same recorded noise
realization; $2\times 10^6$ selection injections share the statistic and threshold
and are reweighted across trial $H_0$; selection $N_{\rm eff} > 12{,}000$ across the
whole grid). Machinery closure gates passed before reading results: a fully
generator-native mock recovers $H_0 = 67.0$ (truth $67.74$), and the complete-catalog
gate on the clustered events gives $69.5 \pm 1.4$ ($+1.3\sigma$).

\section{Compatibility and provenance summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item Legacy per-pixel mode remains the \textbf{default} and bit-identical
(pinned by \texttt{test\_z\_depth\_none\_bit\_identical} and
\texttt{test\_per\_pixel\_default\_bit\_identical}); \texttt{c\_mode} is structural
at trace time (a leaf-less sentinel), and a jit-traced integer flag hard-errors
rather than silently selecting a mode.
\item $Q$ tables are stamped with \texttt{c\_mode} and the budget-renormalization
state; consumption fail-closes on any mismatch, and unstamped legacy tables load as
per-pixel with a loud warning.
\item Deferred, in order: mixture-of-Laplace members over a marginal-likelihood
hyperparameter grid (member coverage is still overconfident: $5\% \to 19\%$);
post-renormalization $\pm 7$ clip-railing accounting;
\texttt{--validate\_completion} under aggregate mode; stratified or magnitude-based
$C_{\rm sel}$ for anisotropic-depth catalogs, with the selection parameters sampled
inside the likelihood and entering the selection integral.
\end{itemize}
